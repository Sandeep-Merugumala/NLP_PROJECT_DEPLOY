================================================================================
ARCHITECTURE DECISIONS
================================================================================

Why Thompson Sampling?
  - No hyperparameter tuning (unlike ε-greedy)
  - Naturally balances exploration/exploitation
  - Provably optimal for Bernoulli bandits
  - Converges in 500-1000 reviews per paper

Why Dual Rewards (SM-2 + RL)?
  - Preserves proven SM-2 algorithm
  - RL bonuses are small (+/-0.20 max)
  - Allows A/B testing (same dataset for both)
  - Easy to adjust weights if needed

Why No Neural Network?
  - Thompson Sampling is non-parametric
  - O(n_arms) complexity (negligible)
  - No cold-start problem
  - Interpretable learning signals
